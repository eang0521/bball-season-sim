Multi-Season Sim
GitHub

Games are played possession-by-possession by the real nba-game-sim engine. "Random players" generates fresh prospects and fast-forwards them through 10 simulated seasons before showing you the resulting league. See SEASON-RULES.md for the progression rules, and the Settings tab for games-per-season and other options.

    Settings

    Changes apply the next time you simulate a season.

    Games per Season
    How many games each team plays. 82 matches a real NBA season; lower it to simulate seasons faster.
    Home Court Advantage
    The home team gets +1 to all four ratings for that game.
    Rating Uncertainty
    Apply independent random noise (std dev below) to every player's ratings at the start of each season.
    Noise Std Dev
    Rating points of variance per stat per player.
    3
    Injuries
    Each game carries a chance of an injury; injured players sit out a random number of future games.

    Show the Leaders tab's content after simulating a season.
